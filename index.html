After The Move

DON'T GIVE THEM ALL BACK

Most people finish a move and immediately need somewhere to put the things they never unpacked. Keep as many totes as you want and we'll build the storage around them — overhead rails or shelving, measured and installed before we leave.

Overhead Rails

Steel rails anchor into the ceiling joists and the tote lips slide straight in, so the bins hang above the cars and out of the way. Nothing sits on the floor, and any tote comes down on its own without moving the others.

  • Uses the dead space above the garage door track
  • Rail spacing set to the totes you keep
  • Anchored into joists, not drywall

Shelving Units

Freestanding steel racks sized so a full tote slides in and out at waist height instead of being lifted over a lip. Shelf heights are set to the tote sizes you kept, so you are not paying for air above every bin.

  • Shelf heights matched to your tote sizes
  • Adjustable later as what you store changes
  • Assembled and placed before we drive off

The boxes you packed in become the storage you keep

Everything is already labelled and sorted from the move. Instead of unpacking the off-season clothes, the camping gear and the holiday decorations just to put them into something else, the tote goes straight onto a rail or a shelf and stays there.
